Privacy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Data on your PC

Kaldrix saves your profile name, its identifier and access tokens on your computer, along with instances and game files. They are in the %APPDATA%\Kaldrix folder. Signing out deletes the saved tokens.

Sign-in

You sign in on Microsoft's website. Your password never passes through Kaldrix. Tokens are sent only to Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft services, and to the game when you start it.

Services contacted

To work, Kaldrix connects to Mojang, Modrinth, Adoptium (Java) and the sites of the mod loaders you choose: Fabric, Quilt, Forge or NeoForge. CurseForge will be contacted only once it is enabled. These services see your IP address and what you search for or download, under their own policies.

What we don't do

Kaldrix sends no statistics, shows no ads and sells no data. If AI features are added later, this page will first explain what data they use.

This website

These pages use no cookies or analytics. The hosting provider may log technical data such as your IP address.

Kaldrix Launcher is not an official Minecraft product. It is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
